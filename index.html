About

I have five years of instructional design experience spanning higher education, workforce development, and technical training. My work focuses on creating accessible, engaging curriculum for diverse adult populations—from community college students to formerly incarcerated learners navigating reentry.

I believe strong instructional design treats learners as whole, complex people and gives them the tools to participate fully in their own education. My approach is grounded in adult learning theory, Universal Design for Learning, and a commitment to equity and inclusion from the start.
