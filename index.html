About

Instructional design energizes me — because I've seen what happens when learning experiences have an actual impact.

I've watched as a participant who'd never touched a smartphone in her entire life learned to build websites from scratch. She went on to become a member of the leadership team at a major nonprofit and a published author featured in national media. I've seen adults paralyzed by learning anxiety earn their own "light bulb" moment when everything clicks. In instructional design, success doesn't just feel like a win; it's a reminder about why this work matters.

Over five years in higher education, workforce development, upskilling, and technical training, I've specialized in creating learning experiences for people navigating real barriers. Whether that's educational gaps, life circumstances, systemic inequity, or the all-too-common beast that is learning anxiety. My approach is grounded in adult learning theory, Universal Design for Learning, and the belief that effective instruction treats learners as capable, complex people deserving of thoughtful, equity-centered, and agency-focused design.

I can take overwhelming content or an undefined instructional vision and turn it into something coherent, accessible, and genuinely transformative. I design curriculum that's structured to hold the bar high, but flexible enough to meet learners where they are, so they can lift themselves to that high bar on their own. Whether I'm building a three-month interdisciplinary program from scratch or refreshing existing corporate training, my goal is always the same: help people build skills, confidence, and a sense of what's possible.
